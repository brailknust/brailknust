# BRAIL Product Context

BRAIL is an AI-powered academic planning and student support platform for university students, starting with KNUST and designed to expand later.

The product is not meant to be a generic productivity app. Its core job is to understand a student's academic structure: university, programme, semester, courses, tasks, timetable, study habits, weak areas, and progress. That context should make planning and AI support feel specific to the student's real workload instead of generic advice.

## Core Problem

Students often struggle to:

- Track assignments, quizzes, projects, exams, and personal study work.
- Balance workload across multiple courses.
- Keep consistent study habits.
- Identify and improve weak subject areas.
- Stay motivated through the semester.
- Find useful ways to collaborate with classmates.

## Product Direction

BRAIL should become a centralized academic workspace where students can:

- Manage semesters and courses.
- Track academic tasks and deadlines.
- Plan study time around their timetable.
- Organize learning resources.
- Track weak areas and progress.
- Get AI help grounded in their academic context.
- Collaborate through peer learning and study groups.

## AI Direction

AI features should be context-aware. The assistant should use the student's courses, tasks, active semester, timetable, weak areas, and study goals when giving recommendations.

Planned AI areas:

- Academic assistant chat.
- Personalized study recommendations.
- Smart study and revision planning.
- Weak-area detection.
- Academic insights based on real activity.

## Build Principle

Every migrated feature should reduce the amount of manual academic management a student has to do. If a feature does not use the student's real academic context yet, keep it as a placeholder rather than filling it with static sample data.
